## Done

- Route-section targets support desktop hover and touch click; the keyboard
  slider opens the same card and moves between the visible route sections.
- The compact card reports the nearest current hourly temperature from the
  fixed Basel forecast point within 30 minutes of estimated point arrival. It
  shows only the temperature value and unit. Local shadow is omitted unless
  matching current shade evidence fully covers that section with known
  sunlit/shaded samples. The percentage is labelled as a sampled estimate.
- The card is positioned near its selected point while avoiding visible map
  controls/status elements. Changing route clears the previous point.
- Added browser acceptance coverage for hover, click persistence, keyboard
  changes, route changes, nearest-hour selection, crossing Basel midnight,
  stale/missing forecast, exact local shade arithmetic, and omission for
  unknown or stale shade.
