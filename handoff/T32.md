## Done

- Route-section targets support desktop hover and touch click; the keyboard
  slider opens the same card and moves between the visible route sections.
- The compact card reports the Basel daily mean forecast selected by the
  point's estimated arrival date, remaining distance and ETA. It labels the
  forecast's city-wide daily resolution. Local shadow is omitted unless matching
  current shade evidence fully covers that section with known sunlit/shaded
  samples. The percentage is labelled as a sampled estimate.
- The card is positioned near its selected point while avoiding visible map
  controls/status elements. Changing route clears the previous point.
- Added browser acceptance coverage for hover, click persistence, keyboard
  changes, route changes, arrival-date forecast selection (including crossing
  Basel midnight), missing forecast, exact local shade arithmetic, and
  omission for unknown or stale shade.
